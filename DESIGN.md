# Department of Files design

The scene is a student presenting a tiny, suspiciously official file office on a
classroom projector. A deep green masthead surrounds a bright, high-contrast work
surface. The saturated colour carries the identity; the forms remain easy to read.

## Palette

Primary: `oklch(0.30 0.065 145)`. Background: `oklch(0.18 0.025 145)`.
Surface: `oklch(0.98 0 0)`. Ink: `oklch(0.23 0.025 145)`.
Accent: `oklch(0.46 0.16 30)` (stamp red). Muted: `oklch(0.43 0.025 145)`.
No gradients, glass panels or decorative grid backgrounds.

## Type and shape

Barlow Condensed Bold for the department sign and headings. IBM Plex Sans for
forms, prose and records. Both are self-hosted under the SIL Open Font License.
Square controls and icon housings, straight rules, restrained solid offset shadows.

## Motion and voice

A CSS paper clerk reacts to selecting, submitting and collecting a PDF. Stamps
briefly tilt into place after success. No motion delays a task; reduced-motion
preferences disable animation. Errors stay literal; success and empty states may
make a small joke. Technical explanations live in an expandable classroom map.
